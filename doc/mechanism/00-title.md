# Paper Curator

### From Paper Chaos to Organized Knowledge

**How agentic AI workflows transform our daily research work**

---

## About This Talk

We are AI researchers and engineers. Every day we read papers, share them on Slack, try to keep up with the field. But the volume is relentless -- and the tools we use to manage knowledge haven't kept up.

What if we could build an **agentic system** -- one that autonomously collects, organizes, understands, and connects research papers -- using the very AI capabilities we work with every day?

This talk walks through **Paper Curator**, a system born from real pain points in our daily workflow. For each problem we encountered, we'll see how combining LLMs, embeddings, clustering, and retrieval into **multi-step agentic pipelines** produced creative, practical solutions.
